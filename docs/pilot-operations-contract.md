## Modules, authority and composition

The local operations facade composes three narrow authorities: a source-owned read-only risk source over the existing admission ledger; purpose-separated operations/support storage with current account/session/role checks; and a restricted durable local notification/status journal that remains usable when the application, database or control adapter is unavailable. No caller request supplies expense, stop eligibility, provider-terminal proof or recovery truth.

The risk source reads existing contributions and immutable original policy/rate identities. It does not create another budget, reprice historical obligations, count provider usage as expense, or obtain career text. The participant and routine-support roles cannot call its restricted source or obtain account/Action/dispatch linkage. Operator summaries expose permitted typed aggregates and incident references; manual per-obligation reconciliation remains its separate elevated interface.

Routine support, operations owner and runtime observer are distinct capabilities even when one synthetic identity holds more than one. Owner installation is a privileged explicit local action, not a public bootstrap route. Runtime/observer capability may append validated minimized signals and add containment only; it cannot reopen, correct expense or promote itself. Support can manage only minimized ordinary cases. Current role/session and trusted time are checked after blocking locks and through protected output writes.

Effective containment composes with #290's shared invitation/inference authority, rather than closing its independent result gateway. A declared failure of the ordinary control adapter must not leave the observer's own durable add-only containment ineffective. Missing/unavailable protected authority remains closed. Already reached provider work retains its financial/remote holds; safe retrieval/export/deletion/status remain independently authorized.

No notification success, acknowledgment, health sample or elapsed timer reopens work. Owner resolution records the handling fact; reopening requires current separate #290 verified authorization/reconciliation evidence and fresh containment revision. A later incident invalidates an older reopening acknowledgment. Ordinary support has neither stop nor reopen privilege.

## Risk figures, thresholds and deduplication

The ledger owns every amount and membership. The risk source reports counted gross exposure separately from compatible observed usage, supported estimates, reviewed conservative maxima and attributable bills; these figures are labelled and are **not additive**. Unknown money/difference and remote occupancy are separate from local active Actions and finite Draft rights.

Check global/pilot lifetime, account/grant lifetime, global/account rolling-24-hour and each frozen workflow's Action/attempt spend cap. Retain the existing rolling carry rule for unresolved obligations. An Action/attempt cap uses that Action's original execution policy, not the currently enabled workflow's limits. USD and artificial SYN fixtures never combine.

For a positive integer cap `L`, the first warning amount is the exact integer ceiling of `4L/5`, evaluated without overflow or floating point. Independently expected boundaries include 24,000/30,000 microUSD, 20,000/25,000, 240,000/300,000, 1,200,000/1,500,000 and 8,000,000/10,000,000. At the cap existing atomic admission denies additional expense; above-maximum evidence remains recordable and causes critical containment, even above a ceiling.

Warning identity binds original policy, scope, account/grant or Action/attempt as appropriate, and original rolling-window identity. Repeated unchanged inspection creates no second warning. Numeric observations can refresh the visible figure without treating each denied request as a new incident; severity, newly breached bound, owner handling and closure are material changes. Durable dispatch identity supplies unknown-event deduplication; terminal/compacted survivors cannot turn an old identity into a new send or signal. No 168-hour delay or ordinary-record TTL erases those obligations.

Sampling evidence states its observation time and coverage. There is no claim of hosted continuous coverage or detection of unregistered instances. A bounded/incomplete source sample is an explicit fault, not a reassuring empty result. The full finite local dataset is checked or the operation fails closed; no silent page-limit omission is accepted.

## Incident and notification lifecycle

An incident has a stable opaque identity, original event time, typed source/outcome, severity, owner/response, current state and notification revisions. Diagnostics never receive career text, prompt/result/raw-provider bodies, credentials, raw URLs/query strings, IP addresses, full user agents, contact details or restricted ledger identifiers. Correlation is minimized and incident-scoped.

The independent journal uses explicit owned local storage with restrictive permissions and durable atomic writes. It records intent before invoking the injected fake email sink. The supplied sink is only a local notification seam; no actual address/provider is discovered or contacted. A crash/lost reply after intent is honestly unknown and is not automatically resent. Known failures alone use the bounded original first-attempt schedule; attempts never multiply on replay/restart or a newly named operation.

The persistent operator list remains visible and unacknowledged after notification failure. Database/application/control failure also reaches the independent fake sink/journal, so the database list is not the only evidence. Once the primary store returns, replay reconciles by stable incident/revision without duplicate effects or resetting original times. Authority failure still denies protected operations during the outage. The journal does not claim real mailbox expiry or hosted independence.

Unacknowledged warnings at 24 elapsed hours add invitation and inference containment. Critical incidents preserve required stops until explicit resolution and separately verified reopening. Notification acknowledgment never implies recovery or proves an SLA.

## Cases, special purposes and retention

Ordinary support cases preserve necessary actual synthetic request/message and optional bounded redacted/synthetic example, receipt/ownership/handling status, substantive-response and closure times. Attachments are not an accepted storage class: intake removes/rejects unnecessary complete CV/Profile attachments while retaining the actual request. A case view never joins Profile or financial tables. Voluntary feedback is explicitly optional and gains no grant or inference effect.

Routine diagnostics expire exactly seven elapsed days after each original event. A minimized ordinary case expires exactly thirty elapsed days after its original closure; duplicate close/reload/restart does not re-age it. Open ordinary cases have an explicit weekly necessity review. A recorded separately necessary investigation hold has purpose, restricted access and review date; it is not a blanket indefinite retention switch.

Security/privacy incidents, rights requests, financial/fiscal evidence, deletion/terminal/replay and restricted expense purposes do not inherit these ordinary TTLs. Their adapter/classification and explicit retirement/hold interfaces have no invented archive, legal retention number or automatic key deletion. Unsupported special-purpose handling remains explicit and does not silently relabel a rights/privacy request as ordinary product support. Mailbox/recipient copies remain separately unverified.

## Contact, public status and rehearsal

EN/PT contact and voluntary-feedback surfaces use explicitly configured fake email handoff in the local composition, never inferred live addresses. They do not automatically attach career data or require feedback for access. No public response/resolution promise is displayed. An independent local status publisher/reader exposes only approved confirmed impact, current status and next-update time; no account, incident linkage or private financial detail is public. Default missing channels remain visibly unavailable.

Owner status updates require a specific next-update time while unresolved; a due no-new-information update remains a real publishing task. Closure states verified recovery and continuing limitations without inventing a repair-time promise. Local availability is tested with the main application/database unavailable; that does not establish a hosted external page or notification delivery.

Synthetic rehearsals cover ordinary, weekend/holiday, absence, rights and incident reports with independently expected literal timestamps, ownership, receipt versus substantive response, review/containment, next update and retention. Simulated clock/operator-work values are labelled synthetic; test runtime is not claimed as measured human staffing feasibility. Missing prepared cover pauses before absence without extending grants or lifecycle clocks.
