## Independent fixture and arithmetic oracle

- Default financial limit is 100 SYN integer micro-units at each applicable account/global/provider scope. One dispatch reserves maximum M=60. Default possible-remote capacity is one. Customer fixture grant is three Draft units. Tests requiring several reached legs must explicitly enlarge the fixture envelope; changing the fixture is not production-limit selection.
- One unknown intent contributes C=60, leaves financial headroom 40 and occupies one possible-remote slot. Two competing maxima of 60 cannot both fit into 100; exactly one complete transaction wins. Repeat with each scope independently the limiting scope and other scopes deliberately roomy.
- Contribution is C=max(E when supported, otherwise 0; R). E=35/R=60 gives C=60, headroom 40; it does not give 95. Synthetic accepted evidence reducing R to 35 gives C=35, headroom 65. Attributable bill 40 gives C=40/headroom 60 once. Credible charge 120 gives C=120/headroom -20 and blocks admission. These literals are an oracle independent of the implementation's calculator.
- Total input 10 including cached subset 4; total output 6 including reasoning subset 2: billable counts are 6 uncached + 4 cached + 6 output. Do not add the cached/reasoning subsets again. Rates and ceilings must be independently specified for a numerical-price fixture; token counts alone do not imply a money amount.
- Customer reservation: 3 available -> reserve 1 -> 2 available/1 held. Pre-delivery cancellation or separately authorized synthetic no-delivery closure yields 3 available/0 deducted once. Successful synthetic delivery yields 2 available/1 deducted once; Accept/export/repeated opening add zero effects.
- `t0=2026-10-01T00:00:00Z`. `t0+167h59m59.999s` is ineligible. `t0+168h` is eligible only, with C=60 and possible-remote capacity still held. If an intent claim occurs at t0 but commits at t0+1h, t0+168h is still ineligible: review is no earlier than t0+169h, or a later trustworthy conservative post-commit bound. Unknown commit/clock evidence denies eligibility.
