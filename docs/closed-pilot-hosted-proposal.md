## Runbook, window and rollback

Freeze one date and staffed four-hour window in BRT and UTC. Proposed breakdown from `T0` is below. Separately approved provisioning may consume up to two staffed hours before `T0`, with the public path restricted until the observation window. Cap planned browser/API/observer requests at 1,000 plus 100 attestor requests, transfer at 1 GiB Google outbound and 1 GB Neon outbound, two approved synthetic accounts, one scoped Draft Action, four expected attested sends and one total opening effect. No participant data or real AI activity.

1. **Provisioning gate.** Obtain approval for the exact resources, plan changes, registry import, IAM, secret versions, logging, budgets, retention, bootstrap and migrations enumerated in the approved configuration. With both stops closed, apply Account bootstrap, run the closed job once, verify privileges and prepare the closed revision with invocation restricted. Record immutable revision and compatibility evidence. Test service health, database connectivity and privacy of log routing under the approved gate and test bounds.
2. **Hosted verification gate, `T0–T+90m`.** Obtain separate execution approval for the exact revision/origin, tester and operator access, status/alert/backup/recovery tests, published ingress window and cleanup. Before the first real OAuth callback, prove sensitive fields are absent from persistent logs. Pin closed traffic and execute the closed authentication and negative matrix. Sign-in alone must leave Profile rights unavailable. For two-account save/export and result isolation, authorize the exact synthetic Profile entitlements and denominators in the test-grant record. Pause if that fixture cannot be represented in current policy.
3. **Scoped grant gate, `T+90m–T+150m`.** Require separate owner authorization for the internal Account and grant version, rights, single Action and four stages, expiry and rescission. With both stops asserted, execute reviewed Draft bootstrap and additive job, stage the IAM-protected attestor, then validate and pin the scoped revision. Install the authorized scope under the independent authority admin and read it back. An active operations binding is currently rejected by the hosted scope check and the fixture verifies that required-but-unavailable observation denies Draft. Prove the approved observer/pause arrangement and applicable canonical prerequisites before enabling scoped inference. If the arrangement requires a binding this build cannot recognize, keep scoped verification blocked pending a reviewed compatible build. Exercise the one approved positive and its negatives. Reassert inference stop by `T+150m` or earlier and record denial of every later send. [Hosted scope readiness](../internal/accountstore/hosted_draft.go)
4. **Contained observation and rollback, `T+150m–T+180m`.** Stop test traffic, read back invitation and inference stops against the current database, and expire/revoke scoped authority. Reconcile any in-flight or unknown send/expense/capacity and preserve opening, deletion and hold decisions. Move 100% of main service traffic to the verified named closed revision against current authority. Remove scoped traffic/tags and attestor invocation and deny fresh or held scoped attempts. Verify closed readiness, unchanged schema and Profile pruning, unavailable managed routes and zero new sends/effects. For failed readiness, missing closure evidence or a migration mismatch, revoke public invocation/ingress, hold authority closed and preserve evidence. Recovery from an older backup follows separately approved #295. [Restore design](hosted-restore-drill-design.md)
5. **Cleanup, `T+180m–T+240m`.** Close the public path, remove test OAuth access and revoke sessions, grant and temporary privileges. Inactivate/remove attestor and migration resources, credential versions, disposable database/copies, and unused images as approved. Keep any `#295` copy only under explicit retention authorization with source identity, owner and absolute expiry, proposed at most seven calendar days and subject to the original deletion clock. Record each removal or expiry readback, conditions and owner for exceptions, plus resource and cost readings. Recheck residuals and posted spend within one business day with the provider lag noted and again at any approved copy expiry.

Pause immediately on identity or rights mismatch, sensitive logging, unexpected billable use, unapproved traffic or a single unintended real send, broken isolation, lost operator/observer access, backup failure or missing evidence. The person with independent pause/revocation authority also needs working status/contact access when either provider compute is stopped. Keep the candidate closed on absent approval, expired scope or uncertain authority.
