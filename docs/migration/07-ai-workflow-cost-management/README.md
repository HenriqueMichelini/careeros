# AI workflow cost management — selected design requirements

CareerOS-managed inference must account for provider expense independently of customer Draft allowance, preserve unknown exposure, and restrict the metadata used for admission and reconciliation. This document records **bounded owner-selected design requirements as of 29 October 2026**. It records selected policy only; full migration architecture and implementation readiness remain outside these approvals.

Canonical records: [#249 AI-control decisions](https://github.com/HenriqueMichelini/careeros/issues/249#issuecomment-6511521383), [#258 restricted ledger and lifecycle](https://github.com/HenriqueMichelini/careeros/issues/258#issuecomment-6511528310), and [#242 planning checkpoint](https://github.com/HenriqueMichelini/careeros/issues/242#issuecomment-6511530796). These supersede older Unapproved wording only for the scopes listed below. **#249/#256/#258 and G02/G03/G06 remain open; implementation readiness remains Not ready.**

## Selected requirements

| Decision | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **S07-P03 — unknown provider expense** | Keep the full reserved maximum for possibly sent work counted against CareerOS's spending limits until evidence reconciles it; conservatively count that maximum when the charge cannot be established. Timeout, cancellation, restart, lease expiry and budget rollover cannot free it. Customer allowance remains separate. |
| **S07-P04 — provider-specific accounting** | Account for every actual AI call using the applicable billable categories and model/tier/rate identity. Preserve unavailable data and count each category once. Keep supported usage/rate estimates, conservative maximum accounting and confirmed billed charges distinguishable; a successful response is not an invoice. |
| **G06 — restricted data/access scope** | The ledger design may use account/action/dispatch IDs, bounded provider-request IDs, a keyed input fingerprint for changed-replay detection, numeric policy/usage/expense records and minimal replay/deletion markers. These can identify or link a person. Access is limited to runtime and separately authorized reconciliation roles, outside ordinary diagnostics. Career content, credentials and credential hashes are excluded. A routine-support ledger view is not selected. |
| **Finite review and conservative closure** | After the finite attribution opportunity, only calls **still financially unresolved** may undergo reviewed full-maximum accounting and safe purpose-based compaction. Supported estimates and confirmed charges are not replaced with maxima merely because time passed. Counted expense and replay/restore protection must survive. Late lower charges may become unprovable and remain conservatively overcounted; that trade-off is accepted. |
| **Initial financial review window** | **W = seven elapsed days (168 hours) from each dispatch's durable intent.** Later stages, retries, lookups and restarts do not slide that dispatch's clock. Expiry makes unresolved calls eligible for reviewed closure only: it performs no automatic closure, purge, budget/capacity release or change to customer Action/Retry/result rights. W is neither a universal ledger TTL nor a provider-response promise or support SLA. |

All five selections above are recorded in the [#249 checkpoint](https://github.com/HenriqueMichelini/careeros/issues/249#issuecomment-6511521383); [#258](https://github.com/HenriqueMichelini/careeros/issues/258#issuecomment-6511528310) records their data/access and lifecycle limits.

Reclassification must count an obligation once at every applicable account/global scope. Moving a full unknown reservation into conservative maximum accounting cannot make that money available, reset period carry or claim a confirmed bill. An estimate is not an additional charge on top of the same reservation. A category change proves neither remote completion nor safe physical-capacity release; evidence-supported adjustments still require the unresolved reconciliation/authority design.

## Existing boundaries retained

Retain React/Vite, Go and the selected PostgreSQL account authority; the Profile's application-validated `bytea` representation remains unchanged. Existing Google-only OIDC and opaque-session ownership also remain. These selections do not choose a new provider, hosting plan, region, live credential configuration or recovery mechanism. See the existing [technology/persistence decision](../02-technology-architecture/README.md) and [authentication/session decision](../04-authentication-user-management/README.md).

Preserve existing TypeSafe/input safeguards, fixed generation/support-review contracts, Profile ownership and explicit review/Save. These approvals authorize no automatic inference retry, regeneration, fallback, skipped review or provider-content storage change. Every exposed workflow—including ingestion stages, qualification analysis, Draft, section review and standalone CV—must participate in the eventual approved admission/accounting design. The current [account journey](../../account-first-draft.md) establishes source boundaries, not hosted monetary controls.

Provider expense does not define delivery or customer charging. Preserve [S06-D01–D05](https://github.com/HenriqueMichelini/careeros/issues/255#issuecomment-6380301147): a complete, correctly bound, reviewable and recoverable job-summary/CV/cover-letter/application-answers bundle is required, covering every explicit employer answer or indicating that none was requested. Incomplete/CV-only output has no partial Draft deduction; honest qualification gaps are not themselves generation failures. Confirmed successful opening for review is distinct from Accept. Lost responses recover the same action/result without regeneration. Deliberate Retry of a known failed/incomplete attempt with unchanged inputs stays within a recoverable Action; Generate another is a new Action. There is at most one eligible delivery effect. Confirmed pre-delivery cancellation or terminal unconfirmed-delivery closure cannot acquire a late allowance deduction. Their authority, races and numerical customer deadlines remain separate unresolved work.

The three existing 30-day rules retain their different scopes/start events: deleted career content in recovery copies, active Profile retention after paid access ends, and routine AI-provider content retention. None supplies a ledger TTL. No Draft-content/history retention or never-paid-account lifecycle is selected here. [#258 checkpoint](https://github.com/HenriqueMichelini/careeros/issues/258#issuecomment-6511528310).

## Future verification requirements — specified, not executed

- Exercise all five public routes, direct Functions/aliases and ingestion branches with fake providers. Invalid/missing authority, exhausted policy and storage failure produce zero sends; legitimate bounded positive controls succeed.
- Race two processes for the last budget/capacity slot and reconstruct them after restart. Every reached dispatch remains accounted for; possibly sent work cannot redispatch or lose exposure after crashes, cancellation or period rollover.
- Check the boundary immediately before and at **intent + 168 elapsed hours**. Only unresolved calls become review-eligible; later events do not reset the clock. No automatic close, purge, budget/capacity release or customer effect occurs.
- Preserve missing/incompatible usage as unknown. Verify provider categories, estimates, confirmed bills and conservative maxima without double counting; duplicate or late evidence cannot duplicate adjustments or silently release money.
- Verify account isolation, changed-payload replay rejection, deliberate Retry versus new Action and key-version handling. Deny routine-support direct ledger access and keep restricted linkage/fingerprints out of ordinary diagnostics. Preserve customer rights when an admission capability becomes invalid.
- Compact and restore synthetic records without reviving an old send, losing counted expense/deletion proof, or charging a terminal customer Action. Retrieval performs no inference; repeated confirmed delivery has one effect. These checks do not independently prove the whole-old-system fence.
