# Hosted restore drill design decision

**Decision on 24 November 2026.** The owner approves the #295 drill design in principle. Execution stays deferred until #294 verifies the exact hosted candidate and the owner separately authorizes the frozen drill. Resolve the #293 and #294 dependencies before execution and retain their evidence. ## Scenario and recovery standard

Start with a database rewind while both hosting control planes remain reachable. Restore a deliberately older backup by controlled logical copy into a fresh Neon project with an isolated, quarantined target. Use synthetic accounts and fake AI egress. Exercise a successful recovery and a failure case in which missing evidence or an unproved fence keeps the target closed. Participant admission stays closed in both cases. [October recovery design](research/careeros-october-recovery-decisions-2026-10-27.md#old-system-exclusion)

Recover the exact still-authorized acknowledged Profile saves and delivered Drafts. Use authority independent of the backup to reconcile later deletions and copy-expiry anchors, original deadlines, revocations, terminal delivery, allowance effects, and unresolved expense and capacity. Require zero resends and zero duplicate allowance effects. Invalidate all pre-restore application sessions and pending OIDC transactions before permitting fresh Google sign-in. Missing or unverifiable acknowledged work keeps reopening denied. [Recovery standard and sign-in reset](research/careeros-october-recovery-decisions-2026-10-27.md#recovery-point-and-authentication-reset)

## Old-system exclusion and reopening

Quarantine the candidate, reconcile durable decisions, and verify that the old system cannot read, write, publish, or dispatch across the relevant paths. Include existing direct and pooled database connections, privileged access, already-held work, and attempts to reconnect or restart. Keep the candidate closed if the operator cannot prove exclusion. Require the owner's explicit authorization for the verified candidate and recovery evidence before advancing the serving generation and reopening the scoped test capability. [Approved reopening policy](https://github.com/HenriqueMichelini/careeros/issues/290#issuecomment-6645259323)

The later #294 decision selects Cloud Run and Neon Launch in São Paulo for local candidate preparation. The #295 exercise requires a verified #294 candidate, a pinned recovery configuration, and separate execution approval. Specify observable terminal evidence for old direct, pooled, and privileged paths. Pair project isolation, credential rotation, and traffic removal with actual denial evidence. Neon's documented session-termination privilege covers sessions of roles outside `neon_superuser`, so account for privileged paths explicitly.
