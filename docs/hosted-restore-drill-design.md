## Operator sequence

The timings below are tabletop inputs. Record UTC timestamps for incident declaration, quarantine, raw-point timestamp, archive restore start/end, reconciliation, accepted fence, owner authorization, tested generation advance and scoped reopen. Track rollback and cleanup separately.

1. **Quarantine, `T0–T+10m`.** Declare the selected incident, start the RTO clock, page the approved independent status channel, assert and read back both durable stops, and remove source public traffic/tags. Freeze dispatch and record inflight handles. Pause the attestor and migration/operator automation as appropriate, with an approved method to check already-held capabilities later. Keep target in quarantine with zero general, participant or managed access. Capture source fixture expectations and the independent latest authority watermark under the approved custody before retiring access needed for evidence.
2. **Restore and reconcile, `T+10m–T+55m`.** Verify archive bytes and source and target identities before restore. Recreate the reviewed role restrictions and exact compatible target catalog using target credentials. Prefer an allowlisted logical data restore under the recovery admin in a single transaction. Verify the effective catalog/grants before exposing the runtime. Reject unknown objects, unsafe memberships or schema drift. Prove the archive predates the prescribed later decisions, then reapply them using the canonical bounded reconciliation, lifecycle, pruning and deletion handles. Recover exact still-authorized acknowledged saves/deliveries and reject missing, unverifiable or ambiguous acknowledgements. Preserve the earliest original deletion/closure and copy-expiry clocks, revocations, separate customer terminal/allowance outcomes, unresolved expense and remote occupancy, and replay/dispatch holds. Invalidate all old sessions and pending OIDC transactions. Keep both stops asserted on target.
3. **Fence and verify, `T+55m–T+90m`.** Apply the approved whole-old-system fence. Keep old invocation/route eligibility revoked, remove old transport/application permissions, terminate old database sessions, prevent old identities from reading target credentials, and remove their ability to reacquire old or new runtime roles. Inventory prior privileged sessions and effective control-plane grants. If necessary to make an old endpoint non-restartable by any old principal, retire it using the approved provider-level method after preserving authorized cleanup/reconciliation evidence. Verify each original entrypoint and already-held capability by attempted read/write/publication/dispatch and terminal denial. Use the matrix below. [Neon privileged roles and session restrictions](https://neon.com/docs/manage/roles#the-neon_superuser-role)
4. **Advance and reopen, by `T+120m`.** Present the incident, reconciliation, stop and fence evidence to the owner. Require a time-bound signed decision for the exact recovery revision, origin, policy, current generation and one scoped synthetic capability. Advance only through the approved generation command with verified stops and no surviving protective hold that blocks it. Verify that stale-generation operations are refused. Permit fresh sign-in and authorized retrieval, then exercise the approved scope under fake egress. Maintain the invitation stop and repeat negative tests after reopening. Restore/reconciliation or fence failure, a missing owner decision, a surviving blocker, or crossing an approved time/cost limit keeps reopening denied. [Approved reopening sequence](https://github.com/HenriqueMichelini/careeros/issues/290#issuecomment-6645259323)
5. **Rollback and cleanup, `T+120m–T+180m`.** Reassert scoped stop, withdraw recovery traffic, expire the test scope and verify denial. Rehearse return to the verified closed configuration only against reconciled, current fenced authority. An incompatible artifact, failed rollback or uncertain authority keeps both systems quarantined and the status path available. Measure rollback start/end and result independently of the RTO. Revoke temporary credentials and record all residuals. At any earlier stop, preserve the recovery proofs and use the same closure and cleanup procedure.

| Failed rehearsal | Tabletop injects an invalid backup checksum, a missing current authority record, or an old privileged/pooled path whose denial cannot be proved. The fence evidence must include the old account/origin and direct and pooled session attempts, old job/control paths, captured publication capabilities and dispatch with the independent fake counter. Rotation, traffic removal, time elapsed or compute deletion alone cannot prove the fence. Use designated synthetic roles and independently verified denial receipts before concluding a path is cut off. Prove the selected fence against restart/reconnect for the old principals and keep target closed if the old estate cannot be fully inventoried.
